# Slide Writing Guide and Worked Example

> **EXAMPLE – COPY THE FORMAT, NEVER THE FACTS.** The worked example below is for Lloyds Banking Group (UK). Use it only to match length, tone and structure.

Use this guide at Prompt 7 to write the Slide_Content table. Each key fills a fixed place in `Nature_Deck_Skeleton_EY.pptx`.

## 1. Writing rules

1. Copy the format, never the facts. The worked example is for Lloyds Banking Group (UK). Never reuse its names, figures or wording for another client.
2. Use only figures from the evidence log, the client exposure table or the workbook. If a figure is not disclosed, write "Not disclosed" or "Not split". Never estimate or round differently from the source.
3. Action titles state the finding, not the topic. "Top priorities line up with LBG’s nature-linked lending, except flood risk", not "Priorities".
4. Proof points lead with a figure or a short label in the *_lead field (e.g. "£10bn", "BNG:"), followed by 8 words or fewer.
5. Write money as £12.1bn, £120m, $79bn (no spaces, one decimal place for billions where disclosed). Write percentages as 9.3%.
6. Use UK English and a neutral tone. Never criticise the client; describe gaps as "not disclosed" or "no target yet".
7. Keep every value within its character limit. The master template flags anything too long in the Slide_Content tab.
8. Leave c1_stat and c1_stat_text blank unless you are given a local-currency figure; the workbook fills them automatically.
9. Exposure tiles (t1–t6): largest disclosed exposures relevant to the High and Medium priorities, largest first. Links use the rank numbers from the Scoring table, e.g. "#3 #4 #7".
10. Slide 3 cards are the top four priorities by rank; merge the two water sub-themes into one card if both appear.
11. Speaker notes (*_notes): one source per line, with page or tab references.
12. Return only the Key | Value table, in the same key order as the Slide_Content tab, with no blank keys except the optional ones.

## 2. Field-by-field guide

| Key | Purpose | Max chars | Good (LBG example) | Weak | Why weak |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cover_title` | The deck title on the cover slide. | 70 | Nature priorities for Lloyds Banking Group: UK NBSAP proof of concept | Nature analysis | Too vague: no client, no country. |
| `client_short` | Short name used inside text, e.g. "Why LBG". | 12 | LBG | Lloyds Banking Group plc | Too long for card headings; use the familiar short name. |
| `s1_title` | Action title stating the overall finding (also s2_title, s3_title). | 75 | UK nature policy is creating investment and rules that LBG cannot ignore | Executive summary | A topic label, not a finding. |
| `c1_head` | Heading for the national signal card. | 40 | The UK is putting money behind nature | NBSAP overview | A label; say what the signal means. |
| `c1_stat` | Optional. Leave blank to use the automatic count. | 10 | 34 | Lots | Not a figure. |

| `c1_p2` | The rest of the proof point. | 45 | waste, £6.1bn water, £5.0bn farming | is being made across many areas of the economy | No figures; too vague and too long. |
| `c2_head` | Heading for the regulation and markets card. | 40 | Regulation and markets are following | Regulation | A label, not a message. |
| `c2_stat` | Headline figure for a regulatory or market signal. | 10 | £1bn | Many | Not a figure. |
| `c2_p1_lead` | Lead: regulation name or figure. | 14 | BNG: | Regulation | Name the rule (e.g. BNG, PRA SS5/25). |
| `c2_p1` | What the rule requires, with date. | 45 | 10% net gain mandatory since Feb 2024 | is getting stricter | No requirement or date. |
| `c3_head` | Heading for why the client is exposed. | 40 | LBG is directly exposed | Client view | Does not say why it matters. |
| `c3_stat` | Client headline nature-related exposure, as disclosed. | 10 | £60.0bn | c.£60bn (estimated) | Never estimate; use the disclosed figure. |
| `c3_stat_text` | What the figure is, with scope and date. | 60 | LBG exposure to nature priority sectors (9.3% of Group) | exposure | Missing scope and date. |
| `c3_p1_lead` | Lead figure for a gap or action. | 14 | £365.6bn | Mortgages | Lead with the figure. |
| `c3_p1` | The gap or action in 8 words or fewer. | 45 | mortgages: flood exposure not disclosed | might be a problem because of flooding risk | Speculative wording; state what is or is not disclosed. |
| `s1_found_note` | One line under the top-4 priorities: the nearest miss and the main gap. | 150 | Agriculture (3.9) just misses High. Nature is eligible in LBG’s 2025 financing framework, but no nature-specific targets or reporting are disclosed. | Other areas are also important. | Says nothing specific. |
| `s1_sowhat` | The single message for the client, leading to page 4. | 140 | LBG has the criteria and the deals. The next step is portfolio-level nature targets, risk data and disclosure, where EY can help. | EY can help with many things. | Not specific to the evidence. |
| `t1_value` | Disclosed exposure figure (tiles t1–t6). | 10 | £12.1bn | £12bn approx. | Rounded and approximate; copy the disclosed figure. |
| `t1_label` | Sector name as disclosed. | 45 | Energy and water supply | Utilities and other stuff | Informal; use the disclosed sector name. |
| `t1_links` | Rank numbers of linked priorities. | 14 | #3 #4 #7 | Water | Use rank numbers, e.g. #3 #4 #7. |
| `t1_flag` | Whether the client classes the sector as nature priority. | 20 | Nature priority | Yes | Use "Nature priority" or "Not nature priority". |
| `s2_sowhat` | How priorities and exposure line up, and the biggest gap. | 200 | LBG’s nature priority exposures (£24.8bn across utilities, agriculture and construction) line up with four of the top five priorities. The largest gap is flood risk in the £365.6bn mortgage book. | The chart shows the scores. | Describes the chart instead of the insight. |
| `s3_card1_title` | Short name of the priority (cards 1–4). | 32 | Flood and drought risk reduction | Risk reduction and prevention (DM01) | Codes are not client-friendly; keep it short. |
| `s3_card1_score` | Score from the workbook. | 3 | 4.5 | High | Use the number, e.g. 4.5. |
| `s3_card1_evidence` | What the public evidence shows, with figures. | 110 | Resilience built into Cleve Hill and HARP; mortgage flood exposure not disclosed. | LBG is doing a lot on flooding. | No source figures. |
| `s3_card1_support` | How EY could support, specific to the evidence. | 110 | Physical and nature-related risk assessment of the lending book; resilience finance criteria. | Strategy support | Too vague. |
| `s3_card1_output` | A tangible output. | 60 | Portfolio flood and drought heatmap | Insights | Not a deliverable. |
| `s3_next1` | A concrete next step (s3_next1–3). | 80 | Validate against the LBG Sustainability Report 2025 | Next steps TBC | Not an action. |

Fields with the same pattern follow the same rule: c1/c2/c3 cards share the head, stat, stat_text, p1–p3 and p1_lead–p3_lead rules; tiles t1–t6 share value, label, links and flag; cards s3_card1–4 share title, score, evidence, support and output.

## 4. Self-check before replying

- Every key is present, in the Slide_Content order, and only the optional ones are blank (c1_stat, c1_stat_text, t6_flag if not classified).
- Every value is within its character limit.
- Every figure appears in the evidence log, the client exposure table or the workbook.
- No LBG names, figures or wording appear unless the client is LBG.
- Titles state findings and are 75 characters or fewer.
- Scores and ranks match the Scoring table exactly.
- UK English, neutral tone, "not disclosed" instead of guesses.
- Output is only the Key | Value table.
