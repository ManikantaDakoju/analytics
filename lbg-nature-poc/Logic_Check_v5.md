This is the logic check for Helen's review on 19 November. It covers the LBG pack (`LBG_Nature_Opportunities_v5.0.pptx`), built from `LBG_UK_Nature_Mapping_v5.0.xlsx`.

The question it answers: does the deck hang together? That means:
- the same number is the same everywhere
- the measures are not mixed up
- each conclusion follows from the evidence on the page before it

Every figure below was read from the recalculated workbook. Nothing was typed by hand.
